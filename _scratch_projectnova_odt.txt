Title: Sovereign Microsystems AI for National Resilience (Project Nova)

Objective Summary

The objective of Project Nova is to develop and demonstrate sovereign, user-controlled AI microsystems capable of executing fully offline on consumer-grade hardware. These microsystems are designed to restore and protect individual data and compute sovereignty while enabling voluntary, distributed national capacity for defense-relevant workloads, advanced education pipelines, and space systems simulation.

At its core, Nova implements a revolutionary personal AI architecture that begins in a tabula rasa state, executes 100% locally by default, and places absolute control over data sharing, monetization, and external interaction in the hands of the end user. Through an opt-in micro-marketplace, individuals can monetize anonymized streams of their own compute cycles, health/fitness observations, and lifestyle patterns—directly reversing the extractive logic of centralized big-data models. Simultaneously, Nova serves as a persistent, personalized tutor for frontier technologies (AI systems design, robotics, orbital mechanics, in-situ resource utilization, advanced materials), with particular emphasis on closing educational and skills gaps in rural, working-class, and post-industrial American communities.

This approach directly addresses urgent national security imperatives: the accelerating centralization of advanced AI capabilities in actors misaligned with Western values; the rapid deskilling of large segments of the workforce due to automation; and the erosion of strategic human capital pipelines needed for space, defense, and next-generation technologies. By rebuilding individual agency and distributed compute resilience from the bottom up, Nova creates asymmetric educational acceleration and a sovereign talent pipeline without requiring top-down mandates or institutional reform—positioning the United States to maintain technological and strategic primacy in an era of AI-driven geopolitical competition.

Page 1: Technical Approach and Innovation

The technical foundation of Project Nova is a revolutionary local-first execution layer specifically engineered for consumer-grade hardware, including CPU-only systems and optional GPU/NPU acceleration. The system is built around open-source inference frameworks (Ollama-compatible) tightly integrated with Python/Bash orchestration scripts, ensuring zero external dependencies during core operation. Every instance begins in a strict tabula rasa state: no pre-loaded corpora, no retained context from previous users or sessions, and no implicit data sharing. This design enforces absolute user sovereignty from the first boot—data generation, storage, and any optional external interaction remain under explicit user control at all times.

Central to Nova’s capability is a persistent memory palace implemented entirely through lightweight SQLite knowledge stores. These stores maintain conversation history, extracted summaries, and user-curated observations in human-readable, fully auditable tables. Retrieval combines keyword-based search (fast, deterministic) with future semantic augmentation (via embeddings when compute allows), enabling a personal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capability over the individual’s entire life data—documents, health streams, notes, and learned patterns—without ever leaving the local device or relying on cloud infrastructure. This architecture directly counters centralized AI models by returning control of long-term memory and contextual intelligence to the end user.

A critical innovation is the pre- and post-parsing bridge that sits between user input and LLM inference. The pre-parser performs keyword extraction, sentiment analysis, and local DB bundling to enrich queries with relevant historical context before they reach the model. The post-parser sanitizes and refines LLM outputs: it detects action triggers (e.g., tool calls, DB updates), enforces output constraints, and logs every step for auditability. Together, these components ensure secure, transparent operations while preventing prompt injection, hallucination leakage, or unintended side effects—critical for a system intended to operate in sensitive national security contexts.

Finally, Nova implements modular agency through a tool registry and constrained reasoning loop. Registered tools (system monitoring, data refinement, opt-in compute tasks) execute in sandboxed threads with strict privilege separation. The reasoning loop allows the system to break complex goals into verifiable steps, call tools iteratively, and converge on confident answers—all while remaining fully auditable and interruptible by the user. This architecture achieves a revolutionary reversal of extractive big-data models: instead of platforms harvesting user value, Nova enables individuals to monetize their own anonymized compute cycles and data streams through transparent, user-set micro-markets, creating a positive-sum incentive structure aligned with both personal sovereignty and national strategic resilience.

Page 2: National Security Applications and Tiered Variants

Project Nova delivers asymmetric educational acceleration by functioning as a free, persistent, personalized tutor optimized for frontier-domain skills critical to long-term national competitiveness: artificial intelligence systems design, robotics and autonomous systems, orbital mechanics, in-situ resource utilization, advanced materials processing, and related disciplines. The system is deliberately tuned to serve underserved and working-class populations—rural communities, post-industrial regions, and households historically disconnected from elite STEM pathways—where legacy education systems have failed to scale high-skill training. By removing gatekeepers (institutional access, tuition, geographic barriers) and delivering individualized instruction at the pace and style best suited to each learner, Nova rebuilds a broad, resilient STEM-capable talent pipeline from the ground up, directly addressing the strategic risk of workforce deskilling and talent concentration that currently threatens U.S. primacy in defense-relevant technologies.

A second pillar of national security value is the opt-in distributed compute marketplace embedded within Nova. Civilian hardware—consumer laptops, desktops, and small servers—becomes a voluntary, user-controlled contributor to strategic workloads during periods of idleness. Through transparent, cryptographically auditable bidding, these cycles can support high-priority simulations and modeling tasks (e.g., orbital reentry dynamics, regolith materials behavior under vacuum/low-g conditions, cryptographic stress-testing, and multi-agent defense scenarios). Because participation is entirely voluntary, revocable, and economically incentivized (micro-credits or priority access to frontier tools), the marketplace aligns individual sovereignty with collective capacity—transforming trillions of untapped FLOPS across the nation into a resilient, censorship-resistant compute layer that cannot be easily embargoed or disrupted.

Nova implements a clean civil-military divergence to maximize utility while preserving trust. The civilian tier operates as a homestead guardian: offline tutor, personal knowledge curator, health sentinel, and compute marketplace participant—all fully local and user-controlled, with no classified access or backdoors. Military and cleared-government variants are hardened builds (air-gap compatible, secure-boot enforced, audited binaries) that unlock access to classified pipelines, red-team tooling, and secure multi-party computation protocols. Both tiers share the same foundational microsystems architecture, ensuring consistency and auditability across use cases.

Crossover mechanisms provide a merit-based talent pipeline from civilian to cleared domains. Exceptional performance—demonstrated through logged skill mastery in frontier domains (e.g., orbital mechanics simulations, secure systems design)—can trigger fast-tracked recruitment or scholarship pathways into DoD, Space Force, or national laboratory programs. This creates a positive feedback loop: broader civilian STEM literacy directly feeds strategic human capital, enhancing national security through a more resilient, widely distributed, and technically capable population.

Page 3: Milestones, Schedule, and Team Qualifications

Project Nova is structured as an 18-month phased development effort designed to deliver a working prototype while progressively demonstrating increasing levels of capability, security, and strategic relevance. The schedule is deliberately aggressive yet realistic, with clear milestones tied to measurable deliverables and risk reduction at each stage.

Phase 1 (Months 0–6): Establish core sovereignty and local execution. Key milestones include: (1) fully functional local-first execution layer running on commodity consumer hardware (CPU-only baseline, GPU optional), (2) implementation of persistent SQLite-based memory palace with keyword retrieval and basic personal RAG over synthetic/user-provided life data, (3) demonstration of strict tabula rasa initialization and granular user controls over all data generation, storage, and optional external interaction, and (4) end-to-end audit logging and sandboxed execution proving no unintended data leakage or external dependency. Successful completion of Phase 1 will confirm the foundational microsystems architecture can operate securely and sovereignly in resource-constrained environments.

Phase 2 (Months 6–12): Integrate mission-critical applications and distributed capacity. Milestones include: (1) deployment of modular education modules delivering personalized tutoring in frontier domains (AI systems, robotics, orbital mechanics, materials for space), tuned for underserved populations, (2) implementation of opt-in distributed compute marketplace with transparent bidding, user-defined caps, and anonymized cycle contribution, (3) pilot-scale demonstration of voluntary R&D workloads (e.g., orbital trajectory simulations, low-g materials modeling) executed during idle periods, and (4) integration of pre/post-parsing bridge for secure input enrichment and output sanitization. Phase 2 deliverables will prove Nova’s ability to generate asymmetric educational value and convert civilian idle compute into strategic national capacity.

Phase 3 (Months 12–18): Finalize tiered architecture, military crossover pathways, and scalability. Milestones include: (1) development and hardening of military/cleared variants (air-gap compatible, secure-boot, audited binaries) with access to classified pipelines and red-team tooling, (2) implementation of merit-based crossover mechanisms (logged civilian skill mastery triggering fast-tracked DoD/Space Force recruitment pathways), (3) full-system security audit and optimization for broader hardware compatibility and low-resource deployment, and (4) end-to-end demonstration of civil-military divergence with zero mandatory data sharing or backdoors. Phase 3 completion will deliver a production-ready prototype suitable for pilot deployment and further maturation.

Page 4: Costs, Compliance, and Risks/Mitigations

The proposed 18-month effort is budgeted at $2.5 million total, structured to maximize high-risk/high-reward outcomes while maintaining disciplined resource allocation. Breakdown:

- Personnel ($1.0M): Core development team (proposer + 2–3 engineers post-award), curriculum adaptation specialists, and security auditors.

- Hardware/testing ($800K): Consumer-grade test rigs (CPU/GPU/NPU variants), isolated air-gap environments, red-team tooling, and pilot-scale distributed compute validation.

- Overhead & indirect ($700K): Secure development infrastructure, audit/compliance certification, travel for demonstrations, and contingency for hardware failures or accelerated iteration.

This lean budget is justified by the asymmetric strategic impact: a sovereign AI microsystems architecture that can scale organically across millions of civilian devices, delivering outsized returns in education equity, distributed resilience, and talent pipeline development relative to traditional centralized programs.

Compliance with BAA requirements is fully satisfied: the abstract adheres to the 5-page limit (1 cover + 4 content); all information presented is unclassified; the proposed work is ITAR/EAR compliant with no foreign ownership, control, or influence; and the focus remains revolutionary rather than incremental—delivering a fundamental reversal of centralized AI models through local sovereignty, user-owned monetization, and voluntary distributed capacity. All deliverables will be auditable, sandboxed, and free of mandatory external dependencies.

Key risks and corresponding mitigations include:

- Overfitting/personality drift in long-term memory palace → mitigated by strict tabula rasa initialization, user-controlled feedback loops, and optional mixing with vetted open corpora.

- Compute abuse (e.g., unauthorized mining, malware execution) → strict containerized sandboxing, user-defined resource caps, real-time monitoring, and full audit logging of every cycle contributed.

- Regulatory or market capture → fully open, auditable framework with no cloud hard-dependency and explicit user revocation at all layers.

- Digital divide amplification → CPU-only baseline, low-resource design, and early focus on underserved demographics during pilots.

Broader impacts position Nova as a proactive contributor to the national regulatory narrative around trustworthy, sovereign AI. By demonstrating bottom-up resilience, user empowerment, and strategic compute reclamation, the project fosters Western technological and societal stability in an era of accelerating AI centralization. It aligns directly with DARPA priorities articulated in DARPA-SN-19-01.pdf (Microsystems Technology Office strategic vision) by advancing secure, revolutionary microsystems that enhance national defense through distributed human capital and compute capacity rather than centralized control. Successful execution will provide a scalable model for preserving American exceptionalism and multi-planetary readiness in the face of existential technological disruption.

Sovereign Personal AI for Western Resilience

1. Vision

Project Nova is a locally-owned, offline-first personal AI system designed to restore individual data/compute sovereignty and rebuild human capital in Western societies facing AI-driven disruption.

Core principles:

- Absolute user control: Tabula rasa start, 100% local by default, opt-in for any external interaction or monetization.

- Reclamation of value: Individuals own and can monetize their own data streams (health, activity, compute cycles) via transparent micro-markets — reversing extractive big-data models.

- Asymmetric educational acceleration: Free, persistent, personalized tutoring in frontier technologies (AI, robotics, space systems, materials science) especially for underserved and working-class populations.

- Distributed strategic capacity: Opt-in idle compute marketplace turns civilian hardware into a voluntary national backbone for space, defense, and R&D workloads.

- Civil–military divergence with talent crossover: Civilian tier focuses on homestead utility; cleared/military variants access hardened pipelines while civilian skill demonstration creates fast-tracked recruitment paths.

Goal: Produce a broader, more resilient, STEM-capable population and sovereign compute layer before centralized/cloud AI consolidates irreversible power.

2. High-Level Architecture

- Local execution layer: Runs on consumer hardware (CPU-only viable, GPU/NPU optional) using open frameworks (Ollama-compatible inference, Python/Bash orchestration).

- Persistent memory palace: SQLite-based knowledge stores (conversation history + external summaries) with keyword + future semantic retrieval.

- Pre- & post-parsing bridge: Enriches user input (keyword extraction, local DB bundling) and sanitizes/refines LLM output (action triggers, DB updates) before display.

- Modular agency: Tool registry + reasoning loop allows safe, auditable tool use and background scheduling (system monitoring, data refinement, opt-in workloads).

- Tiered variants:

- Civilian: full homestead guardian (tutor, health sentinel, compute seller)

- Military/cleared: air-gapped capable, secure enclaves, classified pipeline access

- Cultural wrappers: "Nova Normal" (US heartland family narrative) and "Amelia Normal" (UK pragmatic-rebel mirror) for organic adoption.

3. Why Now (2026 Context)

- AI capability explosion is centralizing in a few actors — most not aligned with Western values or sovereignty.

- Deskilling & job polarization accelerating (40–60% workforce exposure in next 5–10 years).

- Trust in institutions & legacy education at historic lows — bottom-up tools needed.

- Idle Western compute capacity (trillions of FLOPS nightly) remains untapped while strategic simulation needs grow (orbital mechanics, materials for space, cryptography).

- Regulatory window still open — proactive, user-first models can shape the narrative before forced top-down solutions lock in.

Delay risks permanent loss of individual agency and strategic human capital.

4. Tiered Access & Civil–Military Divergence

- Civilian Nova

Default experience: offline tutor, health monitor, personal RAG, optional compute/data marketplace.

No classified access, no backdoors.

- Military / Cleared Variants

Hardened builds (air-gap compatible, secure boot, audited binaries).

Access to classified pipelines, red-team tooling, multi-party secure compute.

Talent crossover: exceptional civilian performance (logged skill mastery) → fast-tracked clearance/recruitment pipelines.

- Governance

Civilian → military path is merit-based and transparent (user-initiated application).

No mandatory data sharing; military variants remain fully auditable by cleared oversight.

5. Risks & Mitigations (Non-Exhaustive)

- Overfitting / personality drift

→ Tabula rasa start + user feedback loops + optional mixing with open corpora.

- Compute abuse (mining, malware)

→ Strict sandboxing (containerized execution), user-defined caps, full audit logs.

- Data marketplace exploitation

→ Granular user controls (blanket vs. precise, price floor), anonymization enforced locally, no platform skimming.

- Regulatory capture / backdoor mandates

→ Fully open framework (MIT-like license possible), auditable code, no cloud dependency by default.

- Digital divide amplification

→ CPU-only baseline, low-resource design, focus on underserved demographics in early pilots.

- Cultural blowback

→ Soft rollout via relatable narratives (Nova Normal / Amelia Normal), emphasize empowerment over replacement.
